# 模擬街機廳 · The Last Arcade

AI 時代的最後一間街機廳——一款等角視角的街機廳經營模擬遊戲（公測版）。

*The last arcade in the age of AI — an isometric arcade management sim (public beta).*

## 📖 遊戲說明

### 🎯 目標
- 帶著 NT$500,000 開一間街機廳，賺到第一個目標 NT$500,000 的累計利潤，接著挑戰 100 萬、200 萬……
- 付不出房租時可以申請一次緊急貸款，之後再付不出來就破產，記得留一點周轉金。
- 賺夠了可以在「選單」開分店，但每間店都要付員工薪水。
- 開新遊戲時可以幫街機廳取名（例如「電玩中古街」），分店會依序叫「電玩中古街1號店、2號店…」；之後想改名可以在「選單」換招牌，每間店 NT$15,000。

### 🛠️ 擺設
- 按「建造」打開型錄，裡面有機台、設備、地面裝潢、牆面裝飾、地板和壁紙。
- 兌幣機和服務櫃台是開店的必備設備，請優先購買；還沒買齊前，遊戲會幫你保留買它們的錢。
- 點選擺好的物品可以移動、旋轉或賣出；機台還能取名、開關機、調整投幣金額和難度。
- 經典復古機和雙打復古機購入時可以挑選遊戲（小精靈、打飛機、俄羅斯方塊等）和機身顏色；顏色買了就不能改，遊戲之後可以花錢「換機板」，費用約機台價格的三成，換完機台的人氣會像新機一樣回升。
- 機台只能在開幕前的籌備期，以及每月月結後買賣；裝潢則每天都能調整。
- 回收價：第一次開幕前賣掉，可以全額（100%）拿回，放心多試幾種擺法；開幕後就依折舊計算，第一個月 70%，之後每個月再少一些（最低 20%）。
- 右上角的 ⟲ ⟳（或鍵盤 Q / E）可以切換四個視角，方便看到不同方向的機台正面。

### 🕹️ 營業
- 營業時間 11:00～21:00，遊戲裡一天約 5 分鐘；可以用 ⏸ 1x 2x 4x 調速度，⏭ 直接打烊。
- 每天打烊會日結，每 30 天月結（付房租、薪水、稅金）。
- 「連續營業」可以一次跑 5～30 天，中途機台故障無法維修，開始前先檢查耐用度。
- 「公休」可以休息一天不開店，機台會恢復耐用度，送修的機台也會修好。

### 🎰 機台
- 投幣越貴、難度越高，前期賺得多，但客人會越來越覺得不值得（挫折感上升）而不玩；簡單的機台比較能接受高價。
- 每台機台都有「耐用度」，用完會故障，要花錢送修並停機 1 天；關機或公休可以讓它休息、恢復耐用度。
- 每天打烊後可以幫機台「保養」：比送修便宜、不用停機，每次恢復三成耐用度（每台每天限一次，壽命不會因此延長）。開始「連續營業」前先保養一輪，可以減少中途故障。
- 「壽命」用完就報廢，只能賣廢鐵。
- 點選機台可以看到頭上的血條（耐用度）、投幣和難度。

### ❗ 店裡的狀況
- 櫃台店員頭上冒出紅色「！」：電玩高手來了！高手很少出現，但一坐下就霸佔一台機台 2～3 小時，只投 1～3 次幣。
- 店員頭上冒出「…」：店裡已經很久沒有客人了，想想是不是該換機台、調價格或加強裝潢。

### 👥 員工
- 每間店（包括第一間）都有一位員工，月薪 NT$30,000，每月月結時支付。
- 生意太好、員工太忙時，他可能會要求加薪 NT$1,000～3,000，店越忙要越多。
- 答應就照新的月薪支付；不答應員工會離職，你得花 NT$30,000 重新請人，新員工月薪回到 NT$30,000。
- 新請的員工會做比較久才開口；加過薪的員工則會食髓知味，店的收入繼續成長時還會再要求。

### 💰 收支
- 收入：機台投幣，加上飲料機和扭蛋機的利潤。
- 支出：房租、員工薪水、電費（開機的機台越多越貴）、娃娃機的獎品成本、維修費和稅金。
- 東西賣掉的回收價會隨使用時間折舊，詳見「擺設」。

### 💡 小技巧
- 日報表會提示哪台機台讓最多客人等不到，可以考慮多買一台。
- 裝潢風格一致、分數越高，來客越多、客人越滿意。
- 天氣和每週新聞會影響人潮，看「明日」預報安排休息日。
- 遊戲每天結算後自動存檔；換裝置可以用「選單」的匯出 / 匯入存檔。

## 📖 How to Play (English)

### 🎯 Goal
- Open an arcade with NT$500,000 in savings. Reach your first goal of NT$500,000 total profit, then go for 1 million, 2 million…
- If you can't pay the rent you can take one emergency loan; miss rent again and you go bankrupt, so keep some cash on hand.
- Once you've earned enough, open a branch from the Menu — but every store has to pay an employee.
- When starting a new game you can name your arcade (e.g. "Retro Alley"); branches are numbered "Retro Alley #1, #2…". To rename later, buy new signs from the Menu (NT$15,000 per store).

### 🛠️ Building
- Press "Build" to open the catalog: machines, facilities, floor decor, wall decor, flooring and wallpaper.
- The coin changer and service counter are required to open — buy them first. Until you have both, the game reserves the money to buy them.
- Tap a placed item to move, rotate or sell it; machines can also be named, switched on/off, and have their coin price and difficulty adjusted.
- The Classic and Duo Retro Cabinets let you choose a game (Pac-Man style, shooter, Tetris…) and a body color when you buy them. The color is permanent, but you can pay to "swap the game board" later for about 30% of the machine price — the machine becomes as popular as a new one.
- Machines can only be bought or sold during setup (before opening) and right after each monthly settlement; decor can be changed every day.
- Resale: anything sold before your first opening is refunded 100%. After opening it depreciates: 70% in the first month, then a little less each month (minimum 20%).
- Use ⟲ ⟳ at the top right (or Q / E) to switch between four views.

### 🕹️ Opening Hours
- The arcade is open 11:00–21:00; one in-game day takes about 5 minutes. Use ⏸ 1x 2x 4x to change speed and ⏭ to close for the day.
- There is a daily report at closing and a monthly settlement every 30 days (rent, wages, tax).
- "Run Several Days" plays 5–30 days in a row. Broken machines can't be repaired mid-run, so check durability before you start.
- "Day Off" skips a day without opening: machines regain durability and machines in repair get fixed.

### 🎰 Machines
- Higher coin prices and harder difficulty earn more at first, but players gradually feel it isn't worth it (frustration rises) and stop playing. Easy machines tolerate higher prices better.
- Every machine has "durability". When it runs out the machine breaks down and must be repaired (costs money and 1 day of downtime). Switching a machine off or taking a day off lets it rest and recover.
- After closing each day you can "service" a machine: cheaper than a repair and no downtime, it restores 30% durability (once per machine per day; it doesn't extend lifespan).
- When "lifespan" runs out the machine is scrapped and can only be sold for scrap.
- Tap a machine to see its health bar (durability), coin price and difficulty.

### ❗ Things to Watch
- A red "!" over the clerk: a pro gamer has arrived! They're rare, but once seated they hog one machine for 2–3 hours and only insert 1–3 coins.
- "…" over the clerk: nobody has come in for a long time — consider new machines, different prices or better decor.

### 👥 Staff
- Every store (including the first) has one employee earning NT$30,000 a month, paid at the monthly settlement.
- When business is booming they may ask for a raise of NT$1,000–3,000 — busier stores ask for more.
- Accept and you pay the new wage. Refuse and they quit: hiring a replacement costs NT$30,000 and the new hire starts at NT$30,000.
- New hires wait longer before asking; employees who already got a raise will ask again as revenue keeps growing.

### 💰 Money
- Income: coins from machines, plus profit from vending and gacha machines.
- Costs: rent, wages, electricity (more powered machines cost more), claw machine prizes, repairs and tax.

### 💡 Tips
- The daily report points out which machine made the most customers give up — consider buying another one.
- A consistent decor style and a high decor score bring more and happier customers.
- Weather and weekly news affect foot traffic; check tomorrow's forecast to plan days off.
- The game saves automatically after every day. To move to another device, use Export / Import Save in the Menu.
